The Art of the Smash

At Faith Agashi, we take pride in our craft. Our made-to-order burgers are built differently. Using our signature smashing technique, we create a mouthwatering caramelized, crispy sear that seals in all the juicy goodness within.

Our passion for culinary innovation drives us to continuously experiment with ingredient combinations until we achieve the perfect recipe. From our custom spice blend to every carefully selected topping, each ingredient is chosen for maximum flavor impact.
